Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Draft. This policy is being finalised before launch. Jhilmil is in pre-launch testing.

1. Who we are

Jhilmil is a tool for creators that lets you upload a video once, edit it, and publish it to the social and video platforms you choose. Contact: uduindia@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name when you sign up.
  • Your videos and edits: files you upload, and the captions, titles and descriptions you write.
  • Connection tokens: when you connect a platform (for example YouTube or Facebook), the platform gives us a token that lets us post on your behalf. We never see or store your password for that platform.
  • Basic usage data: technical logs such as errors and upload status, used to keep the service working.

3. How we use it

We use your data only to provide Jhilmil: to store and edit your videos, to publish them to the platforms you selected, and to show you the status of each post. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Google / YouTube data

Jhilmil's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use YouTube access only to upload the videos you ask us to publish and to show you their status. You can revoke our access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Meta (Facebook / Instagram) data

If you connect a Meta account, we use the permissions you approve only to publish the content you choose and show you its status. You can remove Jhilmil from your Meta account settings at any time.

6. Storage and security

Connection tokens are stored encrypted. Uploaded videos are kept only as long as needed to publish them and are deleted automatically after a short period (planned: 7 days) unless you delete them sooner. We use reasonable technical measures, but no system is completely secure.

7. Sharing

We share your content only with the platforms you choose to publish to, and with infrastructure providers that run the service for us (for example cloud storage). They may process data only to provide that service.

8. Your choices

  • Disconnect any platform at any time inside Jhilmil or on that platform.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data by emailing uduindia@gmail.com. We will act on it within a reasonable time.
  • You can ask what data we hold about you and ask us to correct it.

9. India (DPDP Act)

We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may withdraw consent or request erasure at any time using the email above.

10. Children

Jhilmil is not meant for children under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you inside the app.
